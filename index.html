Southern Cubing, LLC strives to elevate and enhance the competitive Rubik's Cube community within the Southern United States.

Competitions

Texas, Louisiana, Oklahoma, Arkansas, and Mississippi host many World Cube Association competitions each year. Competitions are a great way to meet other cubers.
